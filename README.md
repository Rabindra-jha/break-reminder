# Break Reminder

A lightweight Windows app that lives quietly in your system tray and reminds you to take a break every **20 minutes** — protecting your eyes, posture, and energy throughout the day.

---

## Download

👉 **[Download BreakReminder.exe](../../releases/latest)**

> No installation needed. Just download and double-click to run.

---

## What it does

Every 20 minutes a full-screen reminder pops up with three simple nudges:

| | Tip | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 👀 | **20-20-20 Rule** | Look 20 ft away for 20 seconds to reduce eye strain |
| 🚶 | **Stretch** | Stand up and move to avoid stiffness |
| 💧 | **Hydrate** | Drink a glass of water to stay focused |

Click **"I'm taking a break ✓"** (or press Enter / Escape) when you're ready to continue. The timer resets automatically.

---

## How to use

1. **Download** `BreakReminder.exe` from the link above
2. **Double-click** to run — a green clock icon will appear in your system tray (bottom-right corner of your taskbar)
3. That's it. It runs silently in the background

**To quit:** right-click the tray icon → **Quit**

**To test the reminder instantly:** right-click the tray icon → **Show Alert Now**

---

## Notes

- Works on **Windows 10 and Windows 11**
- No installation, no admin rights required
- To make it start automatically with Windows, place a shortcut to `BreakReminder.exe` in your Startup folder (`Win + R` → type `shell:startup` → paste shortcut there)
